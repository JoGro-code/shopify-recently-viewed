## Set up the gallery

1. Open the Shopify App Store and choose **Free**. [Install Free Gallery](https://recently-viewed-products.fly.dev/go/shopify/en-dawn-client-check) · [Kostenlose Galerie installieren](https://recently-viewed-products.fly.dev/go/shopify/de-dawn-client-check) · [Installer la galerie gratuite](https://recently-viewed-products.fly.dev/go/shopify/fr-dawn-client-check)
2. Open your unpublished Dawn theme in the theme editor. Add **Recently viewed products** under **Apps** on the product template and save. [Watch the 68-second setup](https://recently-viewed-products.fly.dev/watch-shopify-dawn-recently-viewed?locale=en) · [Anleitung ansehen](https://recently-viewed-products.fly.dev/watch-shopify-dawn-recently-viewed?locale=de) · [Voir le guide](https://recently-viewed-products.fly.dev/watch-shopify-dawn-recently-viewed?locale=fr)
3. In Shopify **Products**, open each product and check its **Theme template** field. Choose two products that use the template you configured. Shopify’s assignment list reflects the published theme. If the template exists only in the draft, you can inspect it in the editor and record **Configured** while the storefront test is pending. [Shopify’s product template instructions](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-store/themes/theme-structure/templates#apply-a-new-template-to-a-product)

If you used Shopify Canvas, open the original theme in Shopify’s standard theme editor or create a draft with **Duplicate** from Online Store → Themes. Use that editor to place the RVI block, then follow the same two-product preview test. [Canvas setup in English](https://recently-viewed-products.fly.dev/variant-recovery-setup?locale=en#faq) · [Canvas-Einrichtung auf Deutsch](https://recently-viewed-products.fly.dev/variant-recovery-setup?locale=de#faq) · [Configurer Canvas en français](https://recently-viewed-products.fly.dev/variant-recovery-setup?locale=fr#faq)

## Test the draft with the client

- [ ] Open a fresh browser session, then start the Dawn draft preview in the market you recorded. Allow storefront preferences if Shopify asks.
- [ ] Open product A and then product B in the same browser and draft preview. Check the preview bar on both pages.
- [ ] Find product A in the gallery on product B. Follow its link and confirm it opens the exact product A.

Record **Verified in preview** after all three checks pass. Record **Configured** if you saved the block and the storefront test is pending. If a check fails, record what you saw and the next step. Confirm both product assignments, the preview and the market before repeating the test.

Share the completed sheet privately with the client. For a preview the client can open without Shopify admin access, use **Share preview** in Shopify and share the visitor link privately. [Shopify’s preview sharing instructions](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-store/themes/adding-themes#share-a-theme-preview-with-others)
